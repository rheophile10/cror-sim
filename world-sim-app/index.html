Getting about

Pick somebody in Crew, then click the ground to send them walking there. Everything they could work — switches, crossings, the engine, each car, the station — is ringed with a glowing circle. Walk into one and the ring turns green; the jobs it offers appear under Actions.

To drive: walk to the engine, climb into the cab, then take the controls. Release the automatic brake and wait — on a long train the far end takes a while to let go.

Drag to pan · right‑drag or shift‑drag to orbit and tilt · wheel to zoom.
